# Mini-pitch Q&A (1 minute)

The 10 most likely jury questions, with answers short enough for one minute of questions. Longer answers are in [JURY-QA.md](JURY-QA.md).

| # | Juror | Question | Answer (say this) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Domain | Who is this actually for? | Deaf and hard-of-hearing people first. Nav's video interpreting is only open weekdays 08–20 and needs an approval first. After that: everyone who hates calling. |
| 2 | Product | Didn't Mio try this and shut down? | Mio was for people who *can* call. For a Deaf person the call is impossible, not just annoying. |
| 3 | Founders | Hasn't someone already built this? | SayAhead built a version in English, with a simulated demo. We call for real, in Norwegian, through phone menus and queues. |
| 4 | Law | Is it legal for an AI to call a business? | Yes. The first sentence says it's an AI. The EU's AI rule isn't Norwegian law until 2027, but we already follow it. The user starts every call, and it never sells. |
| 5 | Law | What do you store? Can I delete it? | No audio, no transcript, only a short summary in an EU database. Profile → Delete my account erases everything at once. |
| 6 | IT/security | What stops someone from abusing it, like prank calls? | Login with a phone number and SMS code, so every call is tied to a real number. Emergency numbers are blocked, plus rate limits and a passcode for real calls. |
| 7 | IT | What happens when it mishears a price or a time? | It never agrees to anything alone. Price, time and booking come back to you as YES/NO, and the key facts are pinned and double-checked. |
| 8 | Marketing | How do you make money? | About 30 kr per call. Our cost is about 3 kr. Deaf users can get Nav's 2,000 kr app grant today. Later, interpreter services and municipalities buy it. |
| 9 | Design | Did you test with real users? | Yes, with one Deaf user. She asked for speech-to-text and text-to-speech in one app, and that's what we built. The app collects feedback after every call. |
| 10 | Founders | What's next if you win? | A Norwegian phone number, a pilot with an interpreter service for evenings and weekends, and the Nav listing. |

**Bonus, team efficiency:** two people, 30 hours, 248 commits, 311 automated tests, a web app and a phone app.

**Rules:** answer in one or two sentences, then stop. If you don't know: «Godt spørsmål, det har vi ikke testet ennå.» Never make up numbers.
